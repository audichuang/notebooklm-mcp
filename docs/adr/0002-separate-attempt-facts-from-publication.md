# Separate attempt facts from publication

Generation Attempt records dispatch, observed NotebookLM status, each finalize side effect, and QA disposition as separate facts; no persisted linear or overall status may replace them. Publication belongs to the episode/feed boundary and explicitly identifies the published attempt and audio content, so changing the active or approved attempt never silently changes what is publicly available.

Finalize records each irreversible side effect independently, including intent, remote identity, outcome uncertainty, and verified postcondition. QA approval is valid only for the recorded artifact, audio hash, protected-facts snapshot, and policy version; changing any of them invalidates the approval.
